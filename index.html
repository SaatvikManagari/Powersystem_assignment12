Power Network Fault Classifier

Enter three-phase RMS phasor measurements to classify normal operation, short-circuit faults and open-conductor faults using two independent approaches: phase-quantity signatures and symmetrical components.

Important: This is an educational, simulation-oriented classifier. It uses transparent heuristic rules, not a certified protection relay. Enter measurements from one measurement point, in consistent units, and use phase-to-neutral voltage phasors. Results depend on thresholds and network grounding.

2. What the two approaches do

Approach 1 — phase signatures

Compares each phase voltage/current magnitude with nominal values. It looks for current surges, voltage depressions, phase imbalance and open-phase current loss. It is intuitive and easy to explain, but may confuse faults with heavy loading or voltage disturbances.

Approach 2 — symmetrical components

Calculates I₀, I₁, I₂ and V₀, V₁, V₂ using Fortescue transformation. Ground faults tend to produce zero-sequence quantities; line-to-line faults produce negative sequence without zero sequence; ideal balanced three-phase faults remain predominantly positive sequence.
